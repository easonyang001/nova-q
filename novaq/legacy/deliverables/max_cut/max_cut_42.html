Problem Statement

Given an undirected graph $G = (V, E)$ with $|V| = N$ nodes and a set of weighted edges $E$, where each edge $(i, j) \in E$ carries a non-negative weight $w_{ij}$, the Max-Cut problem seeks to partition the vertex set $V$ into two disjoint subsets $S_0$ and $S_1$ such that the total weight of edges connecting a node in $S_0$ to a node in $S_1$ is maximized.

Decision Variables

For each node $i \in V$, we introduce a binary decision variable $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$. The variable $x_i$ encodes the group assignment of node $i$, where $x_i = 0$ indicates membership in subset $S_0$ and $x_i = 1$ indicates membership in subset $S_1$. The complete assignment is represented by the vector $\mathbf{x} = (x_0, x_1, \dots, x_{N-1})^\top \in \{0, 1\}^N$.

An edge $(i, j) \in E$ crosses the cut if and only if $x_i \neq x_j$. This condition is captured by the indicator function $x_i + x_j - 2x_i x_j$, which evaluates to $1$ when $x_i \neq x_j$ and $0$ otherwise. The original maximization objective is therefore \begin{align} \max_{\mathbf{x} \in \{0,1\}^N} \sum_{(i,j) \in E} w_{ij} (x_i + x_j - 2x_i x_j). \end{align} Since QUBO solvers minimize energy, we negate the objective to obtain the minimization problem: \begin{align} \min_{\mathbf{x} \in \{0,1\}^N} H(\mathbf{x}) = \min_{\mathbf{x} \in \{0,1\}^N} \left( -\sum_{(i,j) \in E} w_{ij} (x_i + x_j - 2x_i x_j) \right). \end{align} Step 2 --- Constraints. The Max-Cut problem imposes no additional constraints on the binary variables; any assignment $\mathbf{x} \in \{0,1\}^N$ corresponds to a valid partition. Thus, the feasible region is the entire Boolean hypercube. Step 3 --- Penalty Terms. As there are no constraints, no penalty terms are required. The Hamiltonian consists solely of the negated objective. Step 4 --- Combined Hamiltonian. Each node $i$ appears in the first sum for every edge incident to $i$, and in the second sum for every edge incident to $i$. Let $d_i = \sum_{j: (i,j) \in E} w_{ij}$ denote the weighted degree of node $i$. The linear part simplifies to $-\sum_{i \in V} d_i x_i$.
