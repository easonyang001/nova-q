Asset Exchange Cycle Selection Problem

Problem Statement

Let $\mathcal{E} = \{0, 1, \dots, M-1\}$ denote the set of $M$ candidate asset exchanges. Each exchange $i \in \mathcal{E}$ is associated with a positive surplus score $s_i$. A conflict graph $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{C})$ is defined such that an edge $(i,j) \in \mathcal{C}$ exists if and only if exchanges $i$ and $j$ share at least one participant or asset. The objective is to select a subset of exchanges $\mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathcal{E}$ such that no two selected exchanges conflict (i.e., $\mathcal{S}$ is an independent set in $\mathcal{G}$) and the total surplus $\sum_{i \in \mathcal{S}} s_i$ is maximized.
