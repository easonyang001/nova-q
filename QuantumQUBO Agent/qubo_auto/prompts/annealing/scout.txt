Search each needed item SEPARATELY (one query per item): statistics of the areas and the boundaries/coordinates
of the areas usually come from different datasets. Publishers title datasets by their level and content, e.g.
"臺北市各里人口數" (各里 = per village) or "臺北市里界圖" (界圖 = boundary map); use such wording.
Coordinates of statistical areas (census tracts, output areas, planning areas) are usually published once for the
whole country or city under the area type's FULL official name plus "centroids" or "boundaries" (e.g. "census tract
centroids"), not per district: search that name WITHOUT the district, then filter to the district in the data stage.
Geometry is usually on the arcgis or WFS services of the national or city geoportal.
For a named service or brand (e.g. YouBike 2.0), put the city and the brand as published together
("臺北市 YouBike2.0"), and add "即時" / "real-time" when the data are live.
